# Detailed Summary of Akash News Features

## 1. Layout & Structure
- **Header**: Contains the official logo "The Daily Akash" (দৈনিক আকাশ), current date (Dhaka time), and social media integration icons (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).
- **Primary Navigation**: A clean top bar for main categories: প্রচ্ছদ (Home), জাতীয় (National), রাজনীতি (Politics), বাংলাদেশ (Bangladesh), আর্ন্তজাতিক (International), খেলা (Sports), অপরাধ (Crime), বিনোদন (Entertainment), অর্থনীতি (Economy).
- **Mega Menu ('সব')**: A full-screen overlay triggered by the hamburger menu icon, providing access to all subcategories, regional news tags, and special sections.
- **Sticky Notice bar**: A persistent ticker at the bottom of the viewport scrolling the latest news headlines.
- **Footer**: Displays editorial disclosure (Director, Editor, Publisher), office address in Mirpur-12, contact phone/email, and a secondary navigation menu for "Our Family" and "Galleries".

## 2. Types of Content
- **Lead Stories**: Featured news cards with large hero images and headlines on the front page.
- **Category-based Blocks**: The homepage is divided into topical segments (e.g., Sports, Politics, International) for easy browsing.
- **Local News (জেলার খবর)**: Comprehensive coverage of districts across Bangladesh.
- **Multimedia**: Dedicated sections for Photo and Video galleries.
- **Social Media Widget**: Embedded Facebook page plugin for direct interaction.
- **Trending News**: Sidebars listing "Latest" and "Popular" articles.

## 3. Core Functionalities
- **Search System**: A dedicated search tool for finding specific articles.
- **Regional News Filter**: Selection dropdowns for Division, District, and Upazila to navigate news by location.
- **Archive Tool (পুরাতন খবর)**: Allows users to retrieve news from specific past dates.
- **Scroll-to-Top**: A floating action button for quick scrolling.
- **Navigation Shortcuts**: "আরও" (More) dropdown and "সব" (All) full-menu for deep navigation.
- **Theme/Language**: Currently limited to Bengali language; no accessibility toggles (like dark mode or language switch) found.
- **Notice Bar Interaction**: Real-time ticker that links directly to the detailed story.
